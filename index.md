## About Me

I'm an undergraduate at Grinnell College interested in mathematics and writing. My research and expository writing can be found here, as can my [CV]({{site.url}}/assets/files/CV.pdf) (last updated 11/22/26).

## Mathematical Interests

Broadly speaking, I'm interested in geometric objects coming from arithmetic contexts. Things I like to think about include, but are not limited to: anabelian phenomena and Galois theory, cohomology of varieties, schemes, and related spaces, local/global fields and the adèles (*i.e.*, arithmetic à la Tate's thesis), representation stability and FI-modules (or more general FI-objects), and Ehrhart theory. I'm fond of categories.

I am also quite interested in mathematical exposition, especially in the current moment where quality, human communication seems more important than ever.
